Pop Out — Support

Pop Out is a browser extension that lets you pick any element on a page and pop it out as a floating, resizable window — plus, optionally, relay messages from a connected AI assistant into chat when you've set that up yourself.

Need help?

Open an issue on this repository and describe what's happening:
github.com/rickeybennett87-stack/support/issues

Privacy

Pop Out reads and modifies the page you're currently viewing only to perform the pop-out/resize/transparency features you trigger yourself, and to relay text to a local AI connector you configure and run on your own machine. It does not collect, transmit, or sell your browsing data to any third party.
